# ChatGPT Plus vs Claude Pro vs Gemini Advanced: Which AI Chatbot Subscription Is Worth It?

Three premium AI subscriptions now cost roughly the same $20 per month: ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, and Google Gemini Advanced. (Gemini Advanced technically runs $19.99/month through the Google One AI Premium plan, which also bundles 2TB of storage.) For anyone who only wants to pay for one, the decision comes down to what you actually do with an AI chatbot all day.

Here's how the three compare on price, model quality, features, and real-world use cases.

## Pricing and What You Get

| | ChatGPT Plus | Claude Pro | Gemini Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $20/month | $20/month (or ~$17/month billed annually) | $19.99/month |
| Latest flagship model | GPT-5 series | Claude Opus 4.5 / Sonnet 4.5 | Gemini 3 Pro |
| Context window | Varies by model | 200K tokens (1M in beta for Sonnet) | 1M tokens |
| Free tier exists? | Yes | Yes | Yes (Gemini app) |
| Extra perks | DALL·E image generation, Sora video, Advanced Voice | Claude Code, Projects, Artifacts | 2TB Google One storage, Workspace integration, Veo video |

All three offer a free tier, so you can test before committing. The paid tiers mainly buy you access to the newest models, higher usage limits, and premium features.

## ChatGPT Plus: The Generalist

ChatGPT Plus remains the default choice for most people, and it's easy to see why. OpenAI's ecosystem is the broadest of the three:

- **GPT-5 series models** with a reasoning mode for hard math, coding, and logic problems
- **DALL·E** for image generation and **Sora** for short video clips (availability varies by region and plan)
- **Advanced Voice Mode** for natural, low-latency spoken conversation
- **Custom GPTs** and a large third-party app/plugin ecosystem
- **Deep Research** for multi-step web research reports

The trade-off is usage caps. Even on Plus, heavy users hit limits on the most advanced models and features, and OpenAI has repeatedly shuffled which model handles which request. If you want one subscription that does a bit of everything—writing, coding, images, voice, research—ChatGPT Plus is the safest all-rounder.

**Best for:** generalists, students, marketers, and anyone who wants image and video generation in the same app.

## Claude Pro: The Writer and Coder's Pick

Anthropic positions Claude as the thoughtful, careful assistant, and that reputation holds up in practice. Claude Pro stands out in three areas:

1. **Long-form writing.** Claude tends to produce more natural prose with fewer filler phrases, which is why it's popular among writers and editors.
2. **Coding.** Claude models consistently rank near the top on coding benchmarks, and **Claude Code**—Anthropic's terminal-based coding agent—is included with Pro (with usage limits).
3. **Large document handling.** With a 200K-token context window (and a 1M-token beta for Sonnet), Claude can digest entire books, codebases, or contract bundles in one go.

**Projects** let you group related chats with shared context and custom instructions, and **Artifacts** render code, documents, and small apps in a side panel you can iterate on live.

The weaknesses: no native image or video generation (Claude analyzes images but doesn't create them), a smaller third-party ecosystem, and usage limits that heavy users notice during peak hours.

**Best for:** writers, developers, researchers, and anyone working with very long documents.

## Gemini Advanced: The Google Ecosystem Play

Gemini Advanced is less a standalone chatbot and more a layer across Google's products. The $19.99/month Google One AI Premium plan includes:

- **Gemini 3 Pro** access in the Gemini app
- **2TB of Google One storage** (worth about $10/month on its own)
- **Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet**
- **Deep Research** and **Canvas** for drafting and editing
- **Veo** video generation and image tools (with limits)
- Integration with Android and Chrome

That 2TB storage bundle is the killer feature if you already pay for Google One—it effectively cuts the AI cost in half. The 1M-token context window is also the largest of the three, useful for analyzing huge PDFs or entire repositories.

On raw capability, Gemini 3 Pro trades blows with GPT-5 and Claude Opus 4.5 on benchmarks, and it leads on some multimodal tasks. The catch is consistency: Google ships features fast but sometimes deprecates or renames them just as quickly, and the user experience across surfaces (app, web, Workspace) can feel uneven.

**Best for:** Google Workspace users, Android owners, and anyone who wants AI woven into email, docs, and storage rather than a separate destination.

## Head-to-Head by Use Case

**Everyday writing and editing:** Claude Pro edges ahead for tone and natural phrasing; ChatGPT Plus is close behind and more versatile.

**Coding:** Claude Pro and ChatGPT Plus are both strong. Claude Code is a genuine differentiator for terminal-first developers; ChatGPT's canvas and code interpreter appeal more to notebook-style workflows.

**Research:** ChatGPT's Deep Research and Gemini's Deep Research are comparable. Gemini's 1M-token window gives it an edge for synthesizing very large source sets.

**Images and video:** ChatGPT Plus wins by default—DALL·E and Sora are built in. Gemini offers Veo and Imagen but with tighter limits. Claude doesn't generate media at all.

**Voice:** ChatGPT's Advanced Voice Mode is still the most polished conversational experience.

**Value if you already pay for storage:** Gemini Advanced, thanks to the bundled 2TB.

## Usage Limits Matter More Than Benchmarks

Benchmark scores get headlines, but the practical difference between these models on everyday tasks is smaller than the marketing suggests. What actually determines satisfaction is how often you hit a usage cap and how gracefully the app handles it.

All three throttle heavy users—typically during peak hours on the most capable models. If you're a power user who spends hours a day in a chatbot, no single $20 plan may be enough; some professionals stack two subscriptions or step up to higher tiers (ChatGPT Pro at $200/month, Claude Max starting at $100/month, or Google's higher AI tiers).

## The Bottom Line

There's no universal winner, but there is a clear decision rule:

- **Pick ChatGPT Plus** if you want the most versatile single subscription, especially with image and video generation.
- **Pick Claude Pro** if your work is mostly writing, coding, or analyzing long documents.
- **Pick Gemini Advanced** if you live in Google Workspace or already pay for Google One storage—the 2TB bundle makes it the best raw value of the three.

If you're undecided, run the same three tasks through each free tier—a writing assignment, a coding problem, and a document summary. The one that fits your workflow is worth the $20. The one that doesn't, no matter how good its benchmarks look, isn't.
